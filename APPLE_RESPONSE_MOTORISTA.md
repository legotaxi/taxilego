# App Review Response — LegoTaxi Motorista (Guideline 3.2 — Business)

Hello App Review Team,

Thank you for the review. LegoTaxi Motorista is a **publicly available** driver app for
independent taxi/ride drivers in Angola. It is **not** an internal, enterprise, invite-only
or company-restricted app. Any eligible driver can download it, create an account directly
in the app, upload the documents required by Angolan law, and start accepting rides. There
is no invitation code, no employer sign-off, and no organisation-based gating in the app.

Below are the answers to the five questions of Guideline 3.2.

---

**1. Who is the target audience of the app?**

Independent taxi and private-hire drivers in Angola (initially Lubango and surrounding
areas) who want to receive ride requests from LegoTaxi passengers. The app is open to the
general public: anyone who holds a valid Angolan driving licence and a vehicle that meets
local requirements can register and use it. There is no restriction to employees,
contractors of a specific company, or members of a closed group.

**2. Is the app restricted to a specific organisation, and if so, how do users obtain access?**

No. The app is not restricted to any organisation. Access is obtained entirely inside the
app: the driver taps "Criar conta" (Create account), enters their name, phone number and a
password, and the account is created immediately. No invitation, referral code, employee ID
or partner agreement is required. Registration is available to every user who downloads the
app from the App Store.

**3. What is the account verification process, and why is it needed?**

After creating an account, the driver uploads three documents that are legally required to
carry passengers in Angola: national ID (Bilhete de Identidade), driving licence (Carta de
Condução) and criminal record certificate (Registo Criminal), plus a profile photo and
vehicle details. Our team checks that the documents are valid and belong to the applicant
(typically within 48 hours) before ride requests are enabled. This is a legal and passenger
safety requirement for transport services, not a membership or organisational restriction —
every applicant with valid documents is approved. Drivers can create the account, sign in,
view the app and manage or delete their account at any time regardless of verification
status.

**4. Who are the users who will be using this app, and how do they obtain access?**

Members of the general public in Angola who wish to drive for LegoTaxi. They obtain access
by downloading the app from the App Store and registering with their phone number inside the
app. The demo account provided in App Store Connect can be used to review the full driver
experience.

**5. What payment/monetisation model does the app use? Are there in-app purchases?**

The app contains **no in-app purchases and no digital goods**. It is free to download and
free to use for drivers. Monetisation works as follows, exactly as implemented in the app:

- Passengers pay for a real-world transport service (a physical taxi ride). Fares are
  calculated as a base fare of 500 Kz, plus 150 Kz per kilometre and 20 Kz per minute of the
  trip.
- Payment for the ride is made either in **cash directly to the driver** at the end of the
  trip, or from the passenger's in-app **Lego Wallet** balance.
- The Lego Wallet is a record of real money: passengers top it up outside the app through
  Angolan bank/payment channels (Multicaixa Express, Multicaixa reference payment, or cash
  deposit); the request is confirmed by our operations team and the balance is credited. No
  Apple in-app purchase is involved because the balance is only usable to pay for real-world
  transport services, in line with Guideline 3.1.5(a)/3.1.3(e) for physical goods and
  services.
- Drivers receive their earnings through the same wallet and request withdrawals to their
  bank account; LegoTaxi retains a service commission on completed rides.
- Passengers earn 10% cashback of the fare into their Lego Wallet, usable only towards
  future rides.

No subscriptions, no paid unlocks, and no digital content are sold in the app.

---

Additionally, the app includes an in-app **"Excluir minha conta"** option under
Conta (Account) for signed-in users, which permanently deletes the account and all associated
personal data, and a public web page at https://taxilego.lovable.app/eliminar-conta with the
same instructions.
